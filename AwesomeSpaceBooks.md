# Space books

Textbooks and handbooks useful for breaking into space as a security engineer, satellite/systems engineer, or related IT role. Prefer the latest edition. Most titles are commercial — links go to the publisher, not pirated copies. Papers, RFCs, and agency guides stay in [Awesome Space Media](AwesomeSpaceMedia.md).

## Space cybersecurity

| Title | Authors | Year | About | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cybersecurity for Space: A Guide to Foundations and Challenges (2nd ed.) | Jacob G. Oakley | 2024 | Practical intro to spacecraft/ground operations, attack surfaces, and why cyber controls on space systems look different from terrestrial IT. Author also appears in the videos list. | [Springer/Apress](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/979-8-8688-0339-0) |
| Safeguarding Satellite Communications: Security Issues, Challenges, and Solutions | Jianping An, Shuai Wang, Dongming Wang, Xiangming Li, Ruhai Wang (eds.) | 2025 | Engineering-focused SATCOM security: architecture, RF/physical-layer issues, interference, reliability, and mega-constellations. | [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Safeguarding+Satellite+Communications%3A+Security+Issues%2C+Challenges%2C+and+Solutions-p-9781394304295) |
| Resilient Space Systems Design: An Introduction (2nd ed.) | Ron Burch | 2026 | Quantitative resilience for space assets: natural and intentional threats, congested orbits, cyber vulnerabilities, recovery and reconstitution. | [CRC Press](https://www.routledge.com/Resilient-Space-Systems-Design-An-Introduction/Burch/p/book/9781041205456) |
| Handbook of Space Security: Policies, Applications and Programs (2nd ed.) | Kai-Uwe Schrogl (ed.) | 2020 | Large reference on space security policy, applications, and programs, including a chapter on space and cyber threats. | [Springer](https://link.springer.com/referencework/10.1007/978-3-030-23210-8) |
| The Physics of Space Security: A Reference Manual | David Wright, Laura Grego, Lisbeth Gronlund | 2005 | Open technical primer on orbits, satellite vulnerability, and counterspace physics. Dated on programs, still useful for the kinematics. | [PDF](https://aerospace.csis.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/physics-space-security.pdf) |

Forthcoming (not yet a substitute for the titles above): *Practical Cybersecurity for Space* (Bailey, Butler, Oakley; Apress, listed for 2026) is billed as a SPARTA-oriented follow-on for people who already work offense or defense.
