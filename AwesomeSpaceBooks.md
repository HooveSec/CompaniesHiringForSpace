# Space books

Textbooks, autobiographies, and 2026 favorite-list picks useful for breaking into space as a security engineer, satellite/systems engineer, or related IT role. Prefer the latest edition. Most titles are commercial — links go to the publisher, not pirated copies. Papers, RFCs, and agency guides stay in [Awesome Space Media](AwesomeSpaceMedia.md).

2026 roundups still pushing the same core shelf: [Space Odyssey Hub (May 2026)](https://spaceodysseyhub.com/articles/best-space-books-for-adults-2026) and [Skriuwer's exploration list (June 2026)](https://www.skriuwer.com/blog/best-space-exploration-books-2026). New astronaut memoirs from 2026 are in their own table.

## Space cybersecurity

| Title | Authors | Year | About | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cybersecurity for Space: A Guide to Foundations and Challenges (2nd ed.) | Jacob G. Oakley | 2024 | Practical intro to spacecraft/ground operations, attack surfaces, and why cyber controls on space systems look different from terrestrial IT. Author also appears in the videos list. | [Springer/Apress](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/979-8-8688-0339-0) |
| Safeguarding Satellite Communications: Security Issues, Challenges, and Solutions | Jianping An, Shuai Wang, Dongming Wang, Xiangming Li, Ruhai Wang (eds.) | 2025 | Engineering-focused SATCOM security: architecture, RF/physical-layer issues, interference, reliability, and mega-constellations. | [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Safeguarding+Satellite+Communications%3A+Security+Issues%2C+Challenges%2C+and+Solutions-p-9781394304295) |
| Resilient Space Systems Design: An Introduction (2nd ed.) | Ron Burch | 2026 | Quantitative resilience for space assets: natural and intentional threats, congested orbits, cyber vulnerabilities, recovery and reconstitution. | [CRC Press](https://www.routledge.com/Resilient-Space-Systems-Design-An-Introduction/Burch/p/book/9781041205456) |
| Handbook of Space Security: Policies, Applications and Programs (2nd ed.) | Kai-Uwe Schrogl (ed.) | 2020 | Large reference on space security policy, applications, and programs, including a chapter on space and cyber threats. | [Springer](https://link.springer.com/referencework/10.1007/978-3-030-23210-8) |
| The Physics of Space Security: A Reference Manual | David Wright, Laura Grego, Lisbeth Gronlund | 2005 | Open technical primer on orbits, satellite vulnerability, and counterspace physics. Dated on programs, still useful for the kinematics. | [PDF](https://aerospace.csis.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/physics-space-security.pdf) |

Forthcoming (not yet a substitute for the titles above): *Practical Cybersecurity for Space* (Bailey, Butler, Oakley; Apress, listed for 2026) is billed as a SPARTA-oriented follow-on for people who already work offense or defense.

## Autobiographies and memoirs

Still the books people recommend first when they want the job from the inside — flight, mission control, or the factory.

| Title | Authors | Year | About | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carrying the Fire: An Astronaut's Journeys (50th anniversary ed.) | Michael Collins | 1974 / 2019 | Gold-standard astronaut autobiography: Gemini 10, Apollo 11 CMP, and what it felt like to fly the command module while Armstrong and Aldrin were on the surface. | [FSG / Macmillan](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780374537760/carryingthefire) |
| Failure Is Not an Option | Gene Kranz | 2000 | Mission-control memoir from Mercury through Apollo 13, by the flight director people quote when they talk about consoles, teams, and "tough and competent." | [Simon & Schuster](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Failure-Is-Not-an-Option/Gene-Kranz/9781439148815) |
| Riding Rockets: The Outrageous Tales of a Space Shuttle Astronaut | Mike Mullane | 2006 | Unvarnished Shuttle-era astronaut memoir — training, culture, and the years around Challenger. | [Scribner](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Riding-Rockets/Mike-Mullane/9780743276832) |
| An Astronaut's Guide to Life on Earth | Chris Hadfield | 2013 | ISS commander memoir plus how astronauts actually train: "what's the next thing that will kill me?" 2026 favorite-list pick for "what the job feels like." | [Little, Brown](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/chris-hadfield-3/an-astronauts-guide-to-life-on-earth/9780316253031/) |
| Endurance: A Year in Space, A Lifetime of Discovery | Scott Kelly | 2017 | Year-on-ISS memoir (Expedition 43/44, NASA Twins Study). Candid on isolation, the body, and long-duration ops. | [Knopf](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/549529/endurance-by-scott-kelly/) |
| Sharing Space | Cady Coleman | 2024 | Shuttle/ISS astronaut and Air Force colonel on being the only woman on a six-person ISS crew, ill-fitting suits, and holding a mixed team together. | [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/716306/sharing-space-by-cady-coleman/) |
| Skunk Works: A Personal Memoir of My Years at Lockheed | Ben R. Rich with Leo Janos | 1994 | Aerospace-engineering culture memoir: U-2, SR-71, F-117, and how a small classified shop actually shipped hardware. | [Little, Brown](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/ben-r-rich/skunk-works/9780316743006/) |
| Ignition!: An Informal History of Liquid Rocket Propellants | John Drury Clark | 1972 / 2018 | Chemist's eyewitness memoir of hypergols, cryogens, and lab explosions; still the funniest propulsion book, reissued by Rutgers. | [Rutgers University Press](https://www.rutgersuniversitypress.org/ignition/9780813595832) |

## 2026 favorite-list picks

Nonfiction that 2026 "best space books" roundups still put on the shelf. Not textbooks — history, popular science, and the people who did the math.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
